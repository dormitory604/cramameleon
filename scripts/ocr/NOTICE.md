# OCR model provenance

- Formula detection: breezedeus/pix2text-mfd-1.5, published by Pix2Text; ONNX metadata identifies Ultralytics YOLO11m and AGPL-3.0. Upstream: https://huggingface.co/breezedeus/pix2text-mfd-1.5 and https://github.com/breezedeus/CnSTD .
- Formula recognition: breezedeus/pix2text-mfr-1.5, model card MIT. Upstream: https://huggingface.co/breezedeus/pix2text-mfr-1.5 .
- Text recognition: RapidOCR ONNX Runtime package and its bundled PaddleOCR models. Upstream: https://github.com/RapidAI/RapidOCR .

Pinned model revisions and SHA-256 checksums are in models.json. Model weights are installed outside Git; no document is sent to model-hosting services during OCR.
